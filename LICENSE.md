# Terms of use for the Wonmor brand files

The files in this repository, including the Wonmor mark, the lockups, the icons and the brand film, are **not open-licensed**. They are published so that people can refer to Wonmor and the dtwin products correctly. No open-source or Creative Commons licence applies to them, and the fact that this repository is public does not grant one.

## What you may do

You may use the files, unchanged, to refer to Wonmor or the dtwin products, for example in articles, reviews, talks, tutorials and links, provided you follow the [brand guidelines](README.md) (also at [wonmor.io/brand](https://wonmor.io/brand/)).

You may show the brand film in coverage of Wonmor or its products, as long as you do not edit, recolour or re-time it, or add other branding to it.

## What you may not do

- Use the mark, the lockups, the icons or the film as your own brand, or as part of your own product, company, project or domain name.
- Use them in a way that suggests Wonmor endorses you, sponsors you or has a partnership with you.
- Modify them, except for proportional scaling.
